Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, our monthly tutoring packages can be paused or canceled at any time. You won't be charged for the following month after cancellation.

How are tutors vetted?

All tutors undergo a rigorous background check, credential verification, and subject-matter assessment before joining our agency.

How do I access the dashboard?

Upon registration, you will receive login credentials to our secure portal to track sessions and manage billing effortlessly.

What if I'm not satisfied with my tutor?

If you're not completely satisfied with your assigned tutor after the first session, we will reassign a new tutor at no additional cost.
